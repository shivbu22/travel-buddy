Delete your account

Account deletion is available directly in the app: Settings → Your data → Delete account. Confirm the action after the identity check.

Deletion removes your profile, trips, photos, prompts, matches, messages, Journal content, sessions, and notification registrations. Limited safety or legal records may be retained when required, as described in the privacy policy.

If you cannot access the app, email support@travelbuddy.app from the email address or phone number associated with your account. Do not send a password or one-time code.
