Support

Need help with a trip, account, safety report, export, or deletion?

Account help

Open Travel Buddy Settings to test the connection, review verification, export your data, or delete your account. If you cannot sign in, contact us from the phone or Apple account associated with the profile.

Safety

Use Report or Block next to the profile, story, or conversation that concerns you. For an urgent danger, contact local emergency services first.

Contact

Email support@travelbuddy.app. Do not include passwords, verification codes, or private identity-review images in email.
