Terms of use

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Travel Buddy is for adults aged 18 and older who want to find travel connections around a shared trip. By using Travel Buddy, you agree to use an accurate profile and treat other people with respect.

Community rules

Do not use Travel Buddy for harassment, threats, fraud, sexual exploitation, spam, impersonation, discrimination, illegal activity, or requests for money that mislead another person. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to share. You are responsible for the content you post and messages you send.

Safety

Travel Buddy provides verification, report, block, and unmatch tools, but no online service can guarantee another person’s identity or conduct. Take a video call first, meet in a public place, tell someone you trust about your plan, and keep independent transport and accommodation.

User content and moderation

Travel Buddy may limit, hide, or remove content and accounts that violate these terms or create a safety risk. Reports are reviewed through a private moderation process. Blocking hides both profiles and ends matching access.

Account controls

You can export or delete your account in Settings. We may retain limited information when required by law or needed to investigate abuse, protect people, or enforce these terms. Apple-linked account deletion includes an authorization-revocation request.

Contact

Questions or safety concerns: support@travelbuddy.app.
