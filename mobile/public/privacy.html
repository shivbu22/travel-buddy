TRAVEL BUDDY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Travel Buddy helps adults find compatible travel connections around a real destination and trip. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and the controls available to you.

Information we use

  • Phone number or Apple sign-in identity to create and secure your account.
  • Name, age, city, profile photos, prompts, travel preferences, trips, and messages to provide matching and conversations.
  • Optional precise location to confirm a city. Exact coordinates are private and are not shown in discovery.
  • Optional camera and photo-library content for profile photos and private identity review.
  • Optional contacts access stays on your device for invitations; Travel Buddy does not upload your address book.
  • Safety reports, blocks, verification status, device notification preferences, and technical request data to protect the community and operate the service.

How we use it

We use this information for sign-in, matching, personalization, messaging, moderation, fraud prevention, service reliability, support, export, and deletion. We do not sell precise location or address-book data. We do not use the current app for advertising tracking or analytics.

Sharing and storage

Profile and trip information is shown to eligible travelers. Messages are delivered to conversation participants. Private reports and identity-review media are restricted to authorized safety operations. Service providers such as hosting, SMS verification, Apple Sign In, push notification delivery, and media storage process only the information needed for their service.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, withdraw optional permissions, block or report people, export your data, or delete your account from Settings. Account deletion removes the account and associated profile, trips, media, matches, messages, and user-generated content, except information that must be retained for legal or safety reasons. Apple-linked deletion also requests revocation of the stored Apple authorization.

Contact

For privacy, deletion, or grievance requests, email support@travelbuddy.app.
